# Ecosystem, Growth & LP Partnerships Lead

## Mission

Connect LPs, institutions, wallets, custodians, and DeFi partners so AMM Markets' technology becomes real partnerships, liquidity, and long-term usage.

## Responsibilities

- Find and develop relationships with individual LPs, funds, family offices, market makers, and institutions.
- Build the LP pipeline, CRM, onboarding, and follow-up process.
- Develop Safe, Safeheron, MPC-wallet, and custody partnerships.
- Build channels with wallets, aggregators, protocols, research groups, media, and relevant communities.
- Operate X, Discord, Telegram, WeChat, and other appropriate communities.
- Create product education, market observations, technical content, and release communications.
- Collect LP feedback and turn it into product, protocol, and data improvements.
- Track qualified LPs, first use, retention, partner channels, and real trading activity.

## Useful capabilities

- Web3 ecosystem, institutional partnerships, LP, or DeFi growth experience.
- Understands AMMs, concentrated liquidity, fee yield, and trading risk.
- Can communicate with engineers, wallet teams, institutional clients, and communities.
- Has evidence of building channels, onboarding clients, or closing partnerships.
- Can communicate in English with institutional partners and explain technical ideas clearly.
- Can manage content, community operations, and a CRM process.

## What we value

Trust, real partnerships, customer outcomes, and risk awareness matter more than follower counts, degrees, or a fixed career timeline.

## Required boundaries

- Do not promise total LP investment returns.
- Do not present one historical case as a future guarantee.
- Do not publish unverified APR or fee-yield numbers.
- Do not access private keys, sign, or custody funds for LPs.
- Do not change Hooks, fees, or trading policy without the appropriate technical owners.
