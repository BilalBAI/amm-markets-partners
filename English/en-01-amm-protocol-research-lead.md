# AMM Protocol & Research Lead

## Mission

Turn AMM research into safe, testable, and deployable Uniswap v4 protocol and trading mechanisms.

## Responsibilities

- Design and maintain Uniswap v4 Hooks and PoolManager-related logic.
- Research dynamic fees, price bounds, liquidity, and fee mechanisms.
- Define active-liquidity, fee-yield, and LP risk metrics.
- Define protocol constraints for adding, removing, and moving liquidity.
- Use Foundry, fork tests, fuzzing, and invariant testing to verify asset flows.
- Define unsigned transaction and Safe/Safeheron boundaries with the Web3 Platform Engineer.
- Prepare audit materials, deployment checks, and production change notes.

## Useful capabilities

- Can read and write Solidity/EVM code.
- Understands concentrated liquidity, ticks, square-root prices, liquidity, and fee growth.
- Can turn mathematical models into reproducible tests and code.
- Has Python, data-analysis, or simulation capability.
- Can analyze traces, calldata, permissions, and asset flows.
- Has practical judgment around callbacks, reentrancy, token accounting, and failure handling.

## What we value

Working protocol code, tests, research notes, and clear reasoning about failure cases matter more than degrees or a fixed career timeline.

## Not owned by this role

- Daily website development.
- Server and network operations.
- The only final security audit.
- Custody or signing on behalf of LPs.
