# Blockchain & Market Data Platform Engineer

## Mission

Build the central data platform that collects, organizes, validates, and serves on-chain and off-chain exchange data for the website, AMM research, risk analysis, and high-frequency systems.

## Responsibilities

- Ingest blocks, transactions, receipts, logs, and pool events from Ethereum, Base, and other supported networks.
- Maintain ownership indexes, pool state, historical replay, reorg handling, and finality tracking.
- Ingest exchange trades, order books, tickers, funding, open interest, and liquidation data.
- Handle WebSocket snapshots/deltas, sequence gaps, reconnects, duplicates, and out-of-order messages.
- Design raw, normalized, and serving data layers.
- Own the central data server, data warehouse, time-series stores, and data services.
- Build lineage, checksums, latency, completeness, and reconciliation checks.
- Serve fee yield, active liquidity, APR, risk metrics, and research features.

## Useful capabilities

- Ethereum RPC, event indexing, reorgs, finality, and historical data.
- Python and SQL, plus TypeScript, Go, or Rust for services.
- PostgreSQL, ClickHouse/TimescaleDB, Redis, Parquet, and object storage.
- Exchange WebSockets, order books, low-latency ingestion, and REST backfills.
- Data replay, quality checks, and recovery design.
- Clear distinction between fee income, fee yield, pool APR, PnL, and total LP return.

## What we value

Real data pipelines, indexers, exchange-market-data systems, research data platforms, or reproducible analyses matter more than degrees or a fixed career timeline.

## Boundary with infrastructure

This role owns data models, ingestion, transformation, and quality. The infrastructure role owns servers, network, permissions, deployment, backup, and the runtime environment.
